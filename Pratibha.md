## Target Audience:  {#target-audience:}

**Pratibha** is a comprehensive platform designed to cater to a wide range of individuals involved in the job search process. Our target audience includes:

* **Retirees:** Individuals seeking to maintain an active lifestyle and earn supplemental income through part-time employment.

* **HR Professionals:** Human resource managers responsible for recruiting, hiring, and onboarding new employees.  
* **Department Heads:** Managers seeking to fill vacant positions within their teams.  
* **Small Business Owners:** Entrepreneurs looking to expand their workforce and find qualified talent.  
* **Recruitment Agencies:** Agencies specializing in placing candidates in suitable roles.

# 2\. Core Features {#2.-core-features}

## User Profiles:  {#user-profiles:}

## Profile Creation and Updates {#profile-creation-and-updates}

**Pratibha** offers a user-friendly platform for creating and managing comprehensive profiles. Here's a breakdown of the key features:

* **Basic Information:** Users can input essential details such as name, contact information, education, and work experience.  
* **Profile Photo:** Uploading a professional profile photo helps create a positive first impression.  
* **Headline:** A concise headline summarizing the user's professional identity and current role.  
* **Summary:** A brief overview of the user's career goals, skills, and experience.  
* **Skills:** Adding relevant skills to showcase expertise and match with potential job opportunities.  
* **Experience:** Detailing work history, including job titles, companies, responsibilities, and achievements.  
* **Education:** Listing educational qualifications, institutions, and degrees.  
* **Certifications:** Adding any certifications or licenses held.  
* **Projects:** Highlighting personal or professional projects with descriptions and outcomes.  
* **Recommendations:** Requesting and receiving recommendations from colleagues and mentors.

**Regular updates** are crucial to keep profiles accurate and up-to-date. Users can easily edit and add new information as their careers evolve.

## Resume Uploads {#resume-uploads}

Pratibha provides a convenient feature for uploading resumes. Users can:

* **Upload Existing Resumes:** Upload resumes in PDF format  
* **Create Resumes:** Utilize the platform's resume builder to create professional resumes from scratch.  
* **Customize Resumes:** Tailor resumes to specific job applications by highlighting relevant skills and experiences.  
* **Save Multiple Resumes:** Save multiple versions of resumes for different job roles.


## Skills Addition {#skills-addition}

Adding skills is a vital aspect of creating a comprehensive profile on Pratibha. Users can:

* **Search for Skills:** Search from a vast database of skills to find relevant terms.  
* **Add Skills:** Manually add skills that are not listed in the database.  
* **Skill Verification:** Optionally verify skills through certifications or assessments.  
* **Skill Level:** Indicate proficiency levels for each skill (e.g., beginner, intermediate, expert).  
* **Skill Endorsements:** Request endorsements from connections to validate skills.

By effectively utilizing these features, users can create compelling profiles that accurately showcase their qualifications and attract potential employers.

## Job Listings:  {#job-listings:}

Basically job listing are posted by Hiring managers for below objectives

Hiring managers create job listings with several key objectives in mind:

## Attract Qualified Candidates {#attract-qualified-candidates}

The primary objective of a job listing is to attract candidates who possess the necessary skills, experience, and qualifications required for the role. This involves clearly defining the specific technical competencies, industry experience, and educational background that are essential for success in the position. A well-crafted job listing not only highlights these requirements but also effectively communicates the nature of the role and the responsibilities involved.

By providing a detailed and accurate description, the listing acts as a filter, helping candidates assess whether they are a good fit for the position before they apply. This reduces the likelihood of receiving applications from unqualified candidates, allowing the hiring team to focus on those who are more likely to succeed in the role. Additionally, a thoughtfully written listing showcases the company's professionalism and attention to detail, further attracting top-tier candidates who are looking for a role that aligns with their career goals and expertise.

In essence, the job listing serves as the first point of contact between the organization and potential hires. Its ability to accurately convey the requirements and expectations of the role plays a critical role in ensuring that the right talent is drawn in from the outset, setting the stage for a more efficient and successful hiring process.

## Communicate Job Requirements {#communicate-job-requirements}

Clear job descriptions play a crucial role in the recruitment process by offering detailed insights into the responsibilities, expectations, and qualifications required for a particular position. By thoroughly outlining the day-to-day duties of the role, a job description provides potential candidates with a comprehensive understanding of what the job entails, helping them visualize how they would perform in the role and contribute to the organization's goals.

In addition to detailing specific tasks, a well-written job description also sets expectations around performance metrics, reporting structures, and any targets or goals the candidate will be expected to meet. This allows applicants to gauge whether they possess the right skill set and level of experience to excel in the role. By including key qualifications—such as educational background, technical proficiencies, certifications, and years of experience—the job description acts as a self-filtering mechanism. Candidates can evaluate their own credentials and determine whether they align with the job’s requirements before applying.

This self-assessment process benefits both applicants and employers. For candidates, it saves time and effort by allowing them to focus on roles where they have a strong likelihood of success. For employers, it reduces the number of applications from individuals who may not be suited to the position, streamlining the hiring process. Ultimately, a clear job description helps ensure that only candidates who genuinely fit the role apply, leading to better hiring outcomes and a more efficient recruitment process.

## Promote the Company Brand {#promote-the-company-brand}

Job listings serve as more than just a tool for outlining responsibilities and qualifications—they are a strategic opportunity for companies to showcase their culture, mission, and values, thereby attracting candidates who resonate with these aspects. In today's competitive talent market, many candidates seek roles that align not only with their professional skills but also with their personal values and career aspirations. By embedding the company’s culture and mission into the job listing, organizations can appeal to individuals who are motivated by more than just a paycheck.

## Showcasing Company Culture {#showcasing-company-culture}

A well-crafted job listing provides insight into the work environment, team dynamics, and leadership style. It might highlight aspects of the company culture such as collaboration, innovation, flexibility, or inclusivity. For instance, if a company values teamwork and cross-departmental collaboration, this can be communicated by describing how the role interacts with various teams. Alternatively, if a company promotes work-life balance, the listing might emphasize flexible work hours or remote work options. This transparency helps potential candidates determine whether they would feel comfortable and thrive within the company’s culture.

## Communicating the Company’s Mission {#communicating-the-company’s-mission}

In addition to describing the role, a job listing should communicate the broader mission of the organization—what the company is trying to achieve in its industry or community. Whether it's advancing sustainability, pushing the boundaries of technology, or serving a social cause, candidates are increasingly drawn to companies that have a meaningful purpose. By clearly stating the company’s mission, the job listing can attract candidates who are passionate about the same goals and want to contribute to a larger purpose.

## Highlighting Core Values {#highlighting-core-values}

Job listings also provide an opportunity to showcase the organization’s core values, such as integrity, innovation, customer focus, or diversity. These values give candidates insight into what the company stands for and the behaviours that are rewarded within the workplace. For example, a listing that emphasizes a commitment to diversity and inclusion signals to potential hires that the company values a diverse workforce and is actively creating a supportive environment. Candidates who align with these values are more likely to feel connected to the organization’s vision and be motivated to contribute to its success.

By incorporating culture, mission, and values into job listings, companies not only attract individuals with the right skills but also those who share a passion for what the organization stands for. This alignment often leads to stronger employee engagement, higher retention rates, and a more cohesive work environment, as employees feel personally invested in the company’s mission and are more likely to stay and grow within the organization.

## Set Clear Expectations {#set-clear-expectations}

By clearly defining job responsibilities, salary ranges, and work conditions in job listings, hiring managers play a crucial role in managing candidate expectations from the outset, which can significantly reduce misunderstandings and mismatched applications. This transparency helps streamline the hiring process by ensuring that candidates have a realistic and accurate understanding of what the job entails, what compensation to expect, and what the work environment will be like before they apply.

## Defining Responsibilities {#defining-responsibilities}

A detailed description of the role’s duties and responsibilities allows candidates to gain a clear picture of what the job requires on a day-to-day basis. When hiring managers specify the core tasks, projects, and decision-making authority associated with the position, applicants can assess whether they have the right experience and skills to handle the role. This clarity helps to avoid situations where candidates apply for positions they are not fully prepared for, reducing the time and effort spent by both applicants and recruiters on unsuitable matches. Additionally, outlining key responsibilities upfront ensures that candidates understand the scope of the role, preventing future disappointment or disengagement due to unmet expectations once hired.

## Communicating Salary Ranges {#communicating-salary-ranges}

Including a salary range in the job listing sets clear financial expectations for both the employer and potential candidates. This transparency allows candidates to assess whether the compensation aligns with their salary expectations or financial needs before they apply. It prevents misunderstandings and frustrations later in the recruitment process when compensation might otherwise become a point of contention. Moreover, providing salary ranges helps ensure that only candidates who are comfortable with the offered compensation will apply, leading to more fruitful discussions during interviews and salary negotiations.

## Outlining Work Conditions {#outlining-work-conditions}

Work conditions, such as work hours, location (e.g., remote, hybrid, or in-office), benefits, and company policies, are essential details that influence a candidate’s decision to apply for a position. When hiring managers clearly specify work conditions, including flexibility in scheduling, potential travel requirements, or the use of certain technologies, it gives candidates a complete picture of what to expect in the work environment. For instance, if the position involves working overtime, frequent travel, or remote work, this should be mentioned upfront to attract candidates who are comfortable with these conditions. This ensures that the candidates who apply are prepared for the role's specific demands, thus reducing the likelihood of mismatches once the job is offered.

## Reducing Mismatched Applications {#reducing-mismatched-applications}

By setting clear expectations in these areas, hiring managers help eliminate applications from candidates who may be unqualified, misaligned, or unable to meet the job's requirements. When there’s ambiguity in the job description, candidates may either overestimate or underestimate their fit for the role, leading to mismatches that waste time for both the applicant and the recruiter. Conversely, a well-detailed job listing provides clarity, ensuring that only candidates who are genuinely suited to the position apply, thus reducing the volume of irrelevant applications and focusing the recruitment efforts on qualified individuals.

## Improving Candidate Experience {#improving-candidate-experience}

Setting clear expectations also improves the candidate experience. Candidates appreciate transparency and are more likely to view the company positively when the details of the job are communicated openly. This builds trust early in the recruitment process, encouraging candidates to engage more meaningfully with the opportunity and reducing drop-offs due to unmet expectations later in the process.

## Screen Applicants {#screen-applicants}

A precise job listing is an essential tool for hiring managers, as it helps streamline the recruitment process by filtering out unqualified candidates early on, ultimately saving time and resources during the screening and interview phases. When a job listing is clear, detailed, and specific about the qualifications, skills, and experience required for the role, it acts as a natural filter that encourages only well-suited candidates to apply. This precision benefits both the company and the candidates by reducing mismatched applications and enabling a more efficient hiring process.

## Setting Clear Expectations for Candidates {#setting-clear-expectations-for-candidates}

A well-written, precise job listing explicitly outlines the core qualifications—such as technical skills, years of experience, educational requirements, and any necessary certifications. This allows candidates to self-assess and determine if they meet the criteria before applying. When job descriptions clearly define must-have qualifications versus nice-to-have skills, candidates who don’t meet the minimum requirements are less likely to apply, thereby narrowing the applicant pool to those who are truly qualified. This specificity deters under qualified candidates from submitting applications, helping to prevent an overwhelming number of irrelevant or inappropriate resumes that can clutter the review process.

## Reducing the Volume of Unqualified Applications {#reducing-the-volume-of-unqualified-applications}

An imprecise job listing may lead to a flood of applications from candidates who are not well-suited for the position, as the lack of clarity can cause candidates to misjudge their eligibility for the role. By providing detailed job requirements, the listing naturally filters out candidates who don’t possess the necessary qualifications, saving hiring managers significant time that would otherwise be spent sorting through unsuitable applications. This efficiency not only accelerates the screening process but also ensures that hiring managers focus their time and attention on evaluating candidates with a genuine potential fit.

Reducing the volume of unqualified applications is essential for helping hiring managers identify candidates with genuinely relevant skills. # Search and Filters: Tailoring Job Listings for Specific User Profiles {#search-and-filters:-tailoring-job-listings-for-specific-user-profiles}

Effective search and filter options on a job platform are essential for connecting candidates with relevant job opportunities and ensuring that hiring managers receive applications from well-matched candidates. By implementing precise filters such as job type, location, industry, and experience level, tailored specifically to each user’s profile, the platform can significantly streamline the search process, improve the quality of matches, and reduce the hit-and-miss ratio for both candidates and hiring managers. Here’s how these tailored filters can improve the job search experience:

### Experience-Level Filters {#experience-level-filters}

Instead of displaying all available jobs that may or may not align with a candidate’s experience, the platform automatically tailors listings to a candidate’s years of experience. For instance, if a candidate has 4-5 years of experience, the platform will only display jobs requiring this specific experience range. This personalized filter eliminates irrelevant job listings that may be too junior or senior for the candidate’s expertise, allowing them to focus solely on roles where they’re more likely to be a strong fit.

For hiring managers, this means that only relevant candidates with the desired experience levels apply, saving time and reducing the need to screen out unqualified applicants. This specificity supports a better alignment of expectations between both parties.

### Domain-Specific Role Matching {#domain-specific-role-matching}

Candidates often come from specialized fields or industries, and displaying job opportunities in other domains can lead to a high rate of irrelevant applications. A personalized filter would align job listings with the candidate’s industry or domain by default. For example, if a candidate has a background in marketing, the platform prioritizes displaying marketing or closely related roles. This domain-specific filter focuses the job search within the candidate’s area of expertise, reducing unrelated job listings and improving the match rate for both candidates and hiring managers.

By automatically aligning job domains, hiring managers benefit from a smaller pool of more qualified applicants who already have relevant experience in the same field, resulting in a higher likelihood of hiring success.

### Location-Based Filtering {#location-based-filtering}

Location preferences are a crucial factor in job searches. By default, the platform can use the candidate’s location (or preferred location if specified) to display nearby opportunities or those that meet certain commuting or relocation expectations. For remote positions, the platform can allow candidates to filter jobs based on their willingness to work in specific time zones or hybrid/remote arrangements. This ensures that candidates are only shown opportunities that align with their location needs, and hiring managers receive applications from candidates who are more likely to be able to commit to the required working conditions.

Location-based filtering minimizes application mismatches and provides a better experience for candidates, reducing the chance of receiving unsuitable offers.

### Job Type Customization (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Remote) {#job-type-customization-(e.g.,-full-time,-part-time,-contract,-remote)}

Job seekers may have specific preferences regarding job types, such as full-time, part-time, freelance, or contract roles. By default, the platform could filter job type preferences based on the candidate’s profile or past job applications. For example, a candidate actively seeking full-time roles will primarily see full-time opportunities, whereas someone with a history of contract work might see a mixture of contract-based roles or projects.

This job-type specificity helps hiring managers by attracting candidates who are already committed to the format and expectations of the job being offered, reducing turnover and enhancing the likelihood of a successful long-term match.

### Industry and Sector Specificity {#industry-and-sector-specificity}

In cases where candidates are searching within sectors—such as technology, healthcare, finance, or education—the platform can refine the job listings to prioritize those sectors by default. For instance, a candidate in the finance industry searching for jobs with a few years of experience will see finance-focused roles tailored to their expertise level and skill set. This sector-specific approach minimizes the display of roles that may be technically similar but irrelevant to the candidate’s career goals, enhancing both relevance and engagement.

For hiring managers, this tailored filter ensures they receive applications from candidates who already have an understanding of the sector’s unique challenges and requirements, reducing the training time and speeding up the onboarding process.

### Skill-Based Filtering {#skill-based-filtering}

As candidates may have distinct skills that suit specific roles, a skill-based filter can refine job search results to match candidates with jobs requiring their unique skill sets. For instance, a candidate with specific software expertise or technical certifications will be shown roles that specifically require those competencies. This skill-based matching helps candidates see roles they’re highly qualified for while giving hiring managers a better chance of receiving applications from candidates with the specialized skills they need.

### Personalization Based on Search and Application History {#personalization-based-on-search-and-application-history}

By analysing a candidate’s past search behaviour, applications, and job interests, the platform can personalize job recommendations to show only roles that align with those trends. For instance, if a candidate consistently applies to mid-level product management roles within the tech industry, the platform can prioritize similar jobs in future search results. This dynamic personalization creates a search experience that evolves with the candidate’s career interests, keeping them engaged and aligned with the most relevant opportunities.

Hiring managers benefit by receiving applications from candidates who have demonstrated consistent interest in similar roles, indicating that they may be a better cultural or professional fit for the company.

### Active and Passive Candidate Matching {#active-and-passive-candidate-matching}

Some candidates actively search for new roles, while others may be passively interested. For passive candidates, the platform could periodically display high-relevance job recommendations based on their profile data, keeping them aware of opportunities that align perfectly with their experience and skills.

For hiring managers, this feature means they’re more likely to reach candidates who may not be actively applying but are well-matched to the role, increasing the likelihood of finding a candidate with niche expertise or a strong cultural fit.

### Reducing the "Hit and Miss" Ratio with Advanced Filtering {#reducing-the-"hit-and-miss"-ratio-with-advanced-filtering}

Together, these refined filters reduce the “hit-and-miss” ratio for both hiring managers and candidates. Candidates are shown roles that better match their preferences and qualifications, leading to a more satisfying job search experience. For hiring managers, this minimizes the influx of unqualified applications, allowing them to focus on serious, well-matched candidates who have the potential to succeed in the role.

### Adaptive Layout and Breakpoints {#adaptive-layout-and-breakpoints}

* **Feature Description**: Pratibha should automatically adapt its layout based on the screen size, providing a streamlined experience across all devices.  
* **Required Specifications**:  
  * Use CSS media queries to define breakpoints for different screen sizes (e.g., small, medium, large).  
  * Define specific breakpoints for:  
    * Desktop (1024px and up)  
    * Tablet (768px to 1024px)  
    * Mobile (320px to 768px)  
  * Prioritize mobile-first design, where smaller screens are the base layout that scales up for larger screens.

  ### Navigation Design for Small and Large Screens {#navigation-design-for-small-and-large-screens}

* **Feature Description**: Pratibha should feature responsive navigation, allowing users to easily access all sections on any device.  
* **Required Specifications**:  
  * **Desktop Navigation**: Use a horizontal navigation bar that displays main categories and sub-menus in a clear hierarchy.  
  * **Mobile Navigation**: Implement a collapsible “hamburger” menu that expands when clicked, displaying key sections in a list format.  
  * Include search and filter options within the navigation for easy access to job listings, personalized profile options, and job management tools.

  ### Optimized Forms and Input Fields {#optimized-forms-and-input-fields}

* **Feature Description**: Ensure all input fields and forms, like job application forms, registration, and job posting forms, are optimized for touchscreens and smaller devices.  
* **Required Specifications**:  
  * Use larger, touch-friendly input fields and buttons for mobile users.  
  * Implement auto-fill and auto-complete for forms to simplify data entry on mobile.  
  * Include “Next” and “Back” navigation for multi-step forms to enhance usability on small screens.

  ### Job Search and Filter Responsiveness {#job-search-and-filter-responsiveness}

* **Feature Description**: Job search and filtering should be responsive and usable across all screen sizes.  
* **Required Specifications**:  
  * Enable collapsible filter panels for mobile, allowing users to show/hide filters as needed.  
  * Provide clear, legible dropdowns and checkboxes that resize appropriately for mobile and tablet.  
  * Optimize real-time search results to adjust based on screen size, with scrollable results on mobile.

  ### Adaptive Image and Media Handling {#adaptive-image-and-media-handling}

* **Feature Description**: Images, graphics, and media within job listings, company profiles, and dashboards should adjust seamlessly across devices.  
* **Required Specifications**:  
  * Use responsive images with `srcset` attributes to load images based on device resolution, minimizing load time on mobile devices.

# Monetization Strategy {#monetization-strategy}

To sustain growth and enhance its offerings, Pratibha will implement a diversified monetization strategy. This approach will include subscription plans, targeted advertisements, and job posting fees, each designed to add value for users while generating revenue. Here’s an in-depth look at these monetization options and how they will benefit both the platform and its users.

## Subscription Plans {#subscription-plans}

* **Overview**: Offer premium features through subscription plans, providing advanced tools and capabilities for both job seekers and hiring managers in exchange for a recurring fee.  
* **Benefits**:  
  * **Enhanced User Experience**: Subscribers gain access to advanced features like detailed candidate analytics, AI-powered job recommendations, priority support, and access to exclusive job listings. These features can help hiring managers reach top candidates more effectively and assist job seekers in gaining a competitive edge.  
  * **Scalability**: Subscription plans allow Pratibha to build a steady revenue stream that can be reinvested into further development and feature expansion, directly improving user experience.  
* **Examples of Premium Features**:  
  * **For Job Seekers**: Access to in-depth career insights, resume-building tools, personalized coaching, and exclusive access to premium job listings.  
  * **For Hiring Managers**: Advanced applicant tracking, enhanced candidate filtering, branded job postings, and priority customer support.

## Advertisements {#advertisements}

* **Overview**: Display targeted, relevant ads within the platform to generate ad revenue. Ads could be related to career development, educational programs, certification courses, and professional services.  
* **Benefits**:  
  * **User Relevance**: By limiting advertisements to relevant and valuable offerings, such as online courses, certification programs, or career development resources, Pratibha can enhance users’ professional growth rather than disrupt the experience.  
  * **Additional Revenue**: Advertising provides a steady, scalable revenue stream without imposing costs on all users, allowing the platform to remain free for basic users while still supporting Pratibha’s operational and development costs.  
* **Types of Advertisements**:  
  * **Promotional Banners**: Featured banners that highlight professional tools, career fairs, or partner programs.  
  * **Sponsored Job Listings**: Allow job postings to be sponsored for greater visibility in search results, benefitting companies looking to fill roles quickly.  
  * **Educational Partnerships**: Ads for educational institutions or certification providers, especially those targeting users seeking upskilling or reskilling opportunities.

## Job Posting Fees {#job-posting-fees}

* **Overview**: Charge hiring managers and companies a fee for posting job listings. This fee can be tiered based on factors like listing duration, prominence in search results, and additional features.  
* **Benefits**:  
  * **Quality Control**: Charging for job postings helps filter out spam and irrelevant listings, ensuring higher-quality opportunities on the platform. This approach is particularly useful for building a trusted space where both job seekers and employers find valuable matches.  
  * **Enhanced Visibility Options**: Companies can choose different posting packages, such as basic listings or premium listings that appear higher in search results or remain active for longer, allowing them to find candidates faster.  
* **Pricing Options**:  
  * **Basic Listing**: Standard posting with limited exposure in job search results.  
  * **Premium Listing**: Featured placement, extended posting duration, and options to add branding or highlight the listing.  
  * **Enterprise Packages**: Bulk listings or special packages for large companies with ongoing hiring needs, offering flexible posting options and additional brand visibility.
